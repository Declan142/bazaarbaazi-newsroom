Nifty traded a 96-point coil between 24,932 and 25,028 through the morning, with weekly writers planting a fortress at the 25,000 strike-cluster.

### The 09:15 to 11:00 walk

The session opened at 24,978, kissed 25,028 in the first nine minutes, then faded straight into a 24,940 print by 09:42. From there the tape locked into a 60-point box for the next eighty minutes. Volumes through the first hour ran 18% below the four-week Thursday average, the classic expiry whisper before the 12:30 IST squeeze.

FII cash provisional read for 2026-05-13 came in at a net sell of ₹1,240 crore, the third straight session of outflow but the smallest in size. DII absorbed ₹1,510 crore, the contra bid doing its quiet work. India VIX opened at 12.42, slipped to 12.18 by 10:30, and that 0.24-point compression was the cleanest tell that writers were comfortable.

BankNifty refused to lead. The index ranged 53,212 to 53,488, a 276-point band against a 96-point Nifty band, but the leadership beta read flat. PSU Bank ran +0.6%, Private Bank gave back 0.3%, the two cancelled. Without the financials sponsoring a directional thrust, the writers had the run of the chain.

The daily candle sits inside the 24,860 to 25,090 horizontal coil that has held since 06 May, the seventh session inside this 230-point sleeve. Wednesday closed 24,985, today's 11:00 print of 24,968 leaves the body smaller than a one-day theta decay. Eight of the last nine candles have bodies under 0.35%. This is not a market resting. This is a market clamped.

BankNifty closed Wednesday at 53,318 and prints 53,344 at 11:00, a 26-point lift dressed as participation. The 200-DMA at 52,140 sits 1,200 points below, the 20-DMA at 53,510 caps the upside by 166 points. The daily ATR has compressed to 412 points from 680 a fortnight back. The financials have stopped voting. The Nifty must settle without them.

### Max-pain anchor

Max-pain at 11:00 IST has settled at 25,000, unchanged from yesterday's 25,000 read but tighter in conviction. The PCR for the weekly chain climbed from 0.94 at open to 1.06 at 10:45, a 12.7% shift that is the cleanest writer-confidence signal of the morning. Call walls and put cushions have rebalanced toward the 25,000 dot with surgical precision.

The 24,950 to 25,050 band now holds 47% of the chain's total open interest, the most concentrated 100-point sleeve since the 04 April expiry. Whatever the close looks like, the magnet is named.

The 12.00 zone has not been visited since 19 March. With realised vol on Nifty running 8.6% annualised over the last ten sessions and the 200-DMA on VIX at 14.95, the option chain is pricing complacency that the cash market has already earned.

### Strike-by-strike defense

**Nifty 25,000 CE.** Opening OI of 1.42 crore contracts swelled to 1.87 crore by 10:45, a 31.7% addition with the spot trading 32 points below. Writers added 45 lakh contracts inside a 50-point spot band. The defense here is not nervous. It is mechanical.

**Nifty 25,100 CE.** OI moved from 96 lakh contracts at open to 1.24 crore by 10:50, a 29.2% build. The strike has not been touched intraday. The 25,100 wall is the breakout invalidation, and the writer who sold at 25,028 earned a 28-point cushion in the first hour.

**Nifty 24,900 PE.** The downside cushion. OI climbed from 1.18 crore contracts to 1.52 crore, a 28.8% build. Notably, the 24,900 PE saw the largest fresh addition between 10:15 and 10:45, the same window the spot dipped to 24,940. Put writers stepped in at the wick, not after.

**BankNifty 53,000 PE.** OI ran from 8.4 lakh contracts to 11.2 lakh, a 33.3% build into a session where BankNifty never traded below 53,212. The financials chain is defending lower, the Nifty chain is defending the round number. Two different stories, one closing print.

### Settlement bias for 15:30

The pinning case is the loudest voice in the room. 47% of weekly OI clustered inside the 24,950 to 25,050 band, a max-pain dot welded to 25,000, and a VIX print under 12.25 all argue for a close between 24,975 and 25,025. The desk's base case is a settlement print inside 24,985 to 25,015, a 30-point sleeve.

The breakout case needs a fresh FII cash buy print on the 13:00 update and a clean BankNifty break of 53,510 to wake up the chain. Without those two, the writers run the last four hours. A close above 25,060 forces the 25,100 CE writer to unwind, and the cascade target is 25,140 by 15:25.

The downside case is thinner. The 24,900 PE writers showed conviction at 10:15. To break that, you need a sub-24,930 print with VIX above 12.60, and neither is on offer at 11:00.

Conviction calibration: pin at 25,000 carries the highest probability, the breakout the second, the breakdown the third.

The 11:00 to 15:30 stretch will reward patience over conviction. Watch the 13:00 FII provisional update, the 12:30 BankNifty volume burst window, and the 14:45 OI flush that has tagged six of the last eight expiries.
